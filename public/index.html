How it works: Naive Bayes computes P(spam | words) ∝ P(spam) × ∏ P(word | spam) using Bayes’ theorem, assuming word independence (the “naive” assumption). Laplace smoothing (α=1) prevents zero probabilities for unseen words. Log-probabilities are used to avoid floating-point underflow when multiplying many small values.
